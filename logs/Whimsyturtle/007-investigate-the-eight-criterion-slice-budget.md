## Decisions and corrections

- Assessment: the concern is partly supported. Mixed independent obligations and cap-aware count-preserving expansion are observable. The evidence does not prove the cap caused every bundle, that any particular bug escaped because of it, or that removing it improves outcomes.
- The human accepted the guideline recommendation and authorized delivery. The [implemented rule](../../workflow/skills/slice.md) keeps reviewability and independent obligations explicit while preserving the other budgets and scope controls. Existing criteria and frozen plans were not rewritten.
- A modest future comparison could record independently identified obligations, reviewer time/clarifications and missed cases for several slices under a softer threshold. This is a recommendation only, not a new plan or accepted requirement.
- Only the slice skill, its new plan and this session summary changed. No application/product-spec/test/dependency change or merge/release occurred. Human log review and merge decisions remain pending.
