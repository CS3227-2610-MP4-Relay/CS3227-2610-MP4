# Test results

Planning area `RES`; RES-1 specifies read-only tester availability and is not implemented yet. See the [product spec](../PRODUCT-SPEC.md), [tester routes](../TECHNICAL-SPEC.md#tester-routes) and [access controls](../SECURITY-SPEC.md#sign-in-and-access-control).

## RES-1 See the tests available to me

A Beta Tester can read the Open and Closed rounds in their own workspace and the instructions needed to test each case against its current build. Closed rounds remain available as read-only reference. Availability is shared by both testers in a workspace; there is no assignment or completion filtering. Draft rounds are unavailable. This requirement does not record outcomes, submissions, observations or history, and does not provide AI feedback.

Each criterion is labelled happy, boundary, failure, adversarial or ambiguous.

- **RES-1.1** (happy) "Tester home" retains the account information and sign-out control of [ACC-1](accounts.md#acc-1-sign-in-and-out-by-role) and shows an "Available tests" section. It lists every Open and Closed round in the signed-in tester's workspace once, by creation time newest first and descending ID when times tie. Each entry is the round title linking to its tester detail page, followed by its state as "(Open)" or "(Closed)". Draft and other workspaces' rounds are absent, including their titles and identifiers. Both testers in a workspace see the same available rounds.
- **RES-1.2** (boundary) With no available rounds, including a workspace containing only Draft rounds, the section says "No tests available." and offers no round links. A workspace containing only Closed rounds lists those rounds as reference.
- **RES-1.3** (happy) Following an available round opens a page with the round title as its page title and heading, "State: Open" or "State: Closed" matching the round's actual state, a "Test cases" section and a "Tester home" link. Each case appears in ascending ID order with its title and the headings "Steps", "Expected result", "Linked acceptance criteria" and "Current build", followed by the corresponding text. Linked criteria appear in ascending ID order and include only that case's links. The current build follows the existing [round definition](rounds.md#round-states): a retest changes the label shown only for cases that build covers, including repeated or overlapping retests; unselected cases keep their previous current build. Reloading after a committed retest shows the new labels, which remain visible after closure. Published-round invariants come from RND-3; this page does not repair missing data or show build history.
- **RES-1.4** (adversarial) On both tester pages, signed-out visitors redirect to sign-in without a redirect target, and Test Coordinators get the fixed 403 of [ACC-1.4](accounts.md#acc-1-sign-in-and-out-by-role), before any round lookup. All workspace record lookups use the shared helper of [ACC-2.4](accounts.md#acc-2-create-an-isolated-demo-workspace). A detail URL naming a Draft, missing or foreign-workspace round gives the same status 404 and fixed "Page not found." page, without its title, state, cases, criteria or build labels. Foreign rounds are unavailable in every state, including Closed.
- **RES-1.5** (adversarial) Both pages accept GET only for Open and Closed rounds; other methods, including HEAD, get 405, except that Django's CSRF middleware first gives 403 to unsafe requests without a valid token or with a foreign Origin, as in [the round action rules](rounds.md#rules-for-every-round-action). Reading or refusing either page changes no round, case, criterion, link or build and creates no tester result. The detail page offers no edit, publish, retest, close or result-entry controls in either state. Existing sign-out remains available on the home page.
- **RES-1.6** (failure) Successful pages, signed-out redirects, 405 responses and storage-failure responses are never cached (`Cache-Control: private, no-store`). A database failure while loading the session or user, reading available rounds or loading detail content returns the fixed 503 page of [ACC-2.5](accounts.md#acc-2-create-an-isolated-demo-workspace), with no partial round content or changes. Session-save failures after the view retain the existing [session persistence behaviour](../TECHNICAL-SPEC.md#data-model).
- **RES-1.7** (adversarial) Round and case titles, steps, expected results, linked criterion text and current-build labels on tester pages follow the existing [escaped plain-text and direction-isolation rules](rounds.md#rules-for-every-round-action), including line breaks and page-title isolation. Markup, attribute-breaking text and stored direction controls cannot execute or escape their text boundary. A round title used as a home-page link follows the same rules. These checks cover coordinator-written text crossing into another user's tester page, including text stored outside the forms.
- **RES-1.8** (boundary) After a coordinator's close has committed, the next home-page GET retains that round with "(Closed)" and the next detail GET remains available with "State: Closed" and the same case instructions and current-build labels. An already displayed page remains until navigation or reload; there is no polling or live update, and a request whose state lookup preceded the close may finish showing Open. Publication makes a round appear on the next home GET with "(Open)".

## Planned work

Later requirements will define saving and submitting outcomes with observations, reviewing previous results and recording retests. The [observation-feedback AI feature](../ai/observation-feedback.md) suggests missing reproduction details.

Before implementation, settle draft ownership, visibility, outcome vocabulary, submission/edit rules, result history and retest links. Retesting may repeat; preserve earlier results and identify the build and criteria they concern. Define concurrent-write and failure behaviour before implementing persistence.
